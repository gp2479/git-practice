# Building Software as a Team Grows

## Article

[How to maintain engineering velocity as you scale](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/how-to-maintain-engineering-velocity-as-you-scale) by **Marcelo Cortes**, published by **Y Combinator**

## What I Find Interesting

This article really stood out to me because I find it fascinating how even though an engineering team can continue to expand, it is possible that they can still work in a coordinated manner and develop software quickly and in a high quality manner. A particular point that I found fascinating was how important it is to write UAT tests early. Although at first it might seem like extra unnecessary work, establishing a strong foundation from the beginning by catching bugs when someone changes code ensures that the entire team can look forward in the future rather than back into the past.

The discussion about **code reviews** was quite interesting too as having other developers check your code is one of the most effective ways to simultaneously catch mistakes while learning. There's more to it than just the coding, however. It is important to ensure all feedback is constructive and respectful, especially when people are still being onboarded to the team. The key lesson is that software engineering requires building good habits in order to write quality code.
